Security

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Security is a core part of doing business responsibly. This page describes the measures EagleTech Studio takes to protect our website and the information you share with us.

Our approach to security

We design, build, and operate our website with security in mind. This means using up-to-date platforms, minimising the data we collect, and applying reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Website security

Our site is served over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit between your browser and our servers. The hosting provider operates managed infrastructure with its own security and uptime controls.

What we don’t do

Our website is a static marketing site. We do not collect passwords, payment card details, or sensitive personal data through this site. If we take payment for services, it is handled through established payment processors under their own security standards.

Third-party services

We use reputable third-party services for specific functions such as hosting and fonts. These providers maintain their own security practices, and we rely on them to protect their part of the chain.

Reporting and vulnerabilities

If you believe you have found a security issue on our website, please contact us. We take legitimate reports seriously and will work to address genuine issues. Please act in good faith and do not attempt to disrupt the site for others.

What you can do

Protect the account or device you use to communicate with us, and be cautious with the messages about our brand to avoid phishing. While we work hard to keep things secure, no website can guarantee absolute security without risk.
