Case Study · Social Media

Safari Yetu — consistency that travels well.

A safari operator selling to international travellers needed social content that looked as premium as the experience itself. The problem wasn’t effort — it was the lack of a system.

The challenge

Safari Yetu Adventures was posting frequently, but every post looked like it came from a different brand. Different fonts, filters, and formats meant the feed didn’t build the trust that international travellers need before booking.

What we did

We designed a complete social media design system: a template library for each content type, a consistent colour and type treatment, photo direction guidelines, and a monthly calendar structure the team could run themselves.

The execution

Instead of designing one-off posts, we built reusable components. Safari Yetu’s team now drops photos into branded templates and produces on-brand content without a designer. We also documented the system so new team members could pick it up quickly.

The outcome

The feed went from inconsistent to unmistakably cohesive. The brand now presents one clear identity across every channel — the trust foundation international travellers look for.
