Case Study · Rebrand

Tronavo — a brand rebuilt to be taken seriously.

An inconsistent visual identity was eroding trust before Tronavo could make a first impression. We rebuilt the entire brand system so every touchpoint finally told the same story.

The challenge

Tronavo had strong service quality but a scattered visual identity. Logo files from three different eras, no colour or type system, and a website that looked like it belonged to a different company every time it was visited. Inbound enquiries were low, and the sales team was spending calls explaining that the brand "was better than it looked."

What we did

We started with strategy, not design. A positioning workshop clarified who Tronavo serves and what it stands for. From there we built a complete identity system: a new primary logo and lockups, a colour and typography system, social templates, stationery, and a 30-page brand guideline document.

The execution

Every deliverable was produced as part of one system rather than a collection of assets. The guidelines document meant Tronavo’s team could produce on-brand material internally without a designer. We also rebuilt their core digital touchpoints so the identity carried through consistently.

The outcome

The full brand system was delivered in six weeks. Tronavo now has a coherent, documented identity they fully own — and a first impression that finally matches the quality of their work.
