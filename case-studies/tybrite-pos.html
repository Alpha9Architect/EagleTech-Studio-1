Case Study · Product Design

Tybrite Labs POS — a point of sale built for real retail.

Retail staff move fast. A POS system that slows them down loses money. We helped design an interface built for speed, simplicity, and scale across East African retail.

The challenge

Existing POS systems were built for contexts very different from East African retail. Checkout flows were too slow, the interface was cluttered, and staff training took too long. Tybrite Labs needed a system that was fast to learn and faster to use.

What we did

We mapped the real checkout flow and rebuilt it around the actions staff perform most. A simplified product interface, quick-sale paths, and clear transaction feedback reduced the steps to complete a sale.

The execution

Design was delivered as a working system the engineers could build against, with a component library covering every screen in the retail journey. We iterated with real staff feedback during development.

The outcome

Transactions became meaningfully faster, and the system has been onboarded across hundreds of retail locations. The interface now works for the people who use it every day.
