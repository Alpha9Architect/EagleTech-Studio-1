Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us: when you contact us through our forms, subscribe to our newsletter, or communicate with us by email, we receive your name, email address, and the content of your message. We do not require an account to browse this site.

How we use information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiries, and to send you requested updates if you subscribe. If you subscribe, you can unsubscribe at any time. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Cookies and site data

This is a static marketing website. It does not set tracking cookies. The hosting platform (Vercel) may collect standard technical logs such as IP addresses and request metadata to operate and secure the service. These are not used to identify you personally.

Third parties

We use Google Fonts to display typography and host imagery from third-party providers. These services may process limited technical data under their own privacy policies. We do not pass your personal information to advertising networks.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have shared with us. To do so, email hello@eagletechstudio.com. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Data retention

We retain enquiry messages only as long as needed to address them, after which they are deleted. Newsletter subscriber details are retained until you unsubscribe.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us against unauthorised access and misuse. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
