Random posts don’t build brands.

Sep 2024 8 min read

Posting randomly fills a calendar. It doesn’t build a brand. Systems are what turn output into identity.

Content without system is noise

A feed of disconnected posts is indistinguishable from a competitor’s. Without a system, volume adds nothing to your identity — it just adds noise.

Patterns are how people remember you

People recognise brands by patterns, not individual posts. The recurring structure, the consistent voice, the familiar look — that’s what sticks.

System first, then volume

Once the system is in place, scaling volume is easy — templates make production fast. Scaling volume without a system just scales the inconsistency.

Review and refine

A design system isn’t static. Review what performs, refine the templates, and keep the system sharp. Consistency that adapts stays fresh.
